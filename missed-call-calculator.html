Why the number is bigger than it looks

A missed call is more than one job

It's that customer's next job too, the referral they would have sent your way, and the review they'll now write about somebody else. The calculator only counts the first sale.

Where the calls actually go

Plenty of people who reach voicemail never leave a message. They call the next name on the list, and whoever picks up first usually gets the work.

What a voice agent does, and doesn't

It answers every call, books the next step on your calendar and sends you the details. It doesn't haggle on price or promise what your team wouldn't, and anything it can't handle goes to a person.
