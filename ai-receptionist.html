Questions owners ask

Will my visitors know it's AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an AI receptionist, and a real person from your team follows up on every lead.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. It offers open times from your calendar and books the appointment inside the chat or call.

Will it work on my website?

It works on most websites, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and Shopify, through a small piece of code. If someone else manages your site, we work with them.

Can it answer my business phone?

Yes, on the Pro plan. You forward after-hours or overflow calls to it, and it answers, books and sends you the details.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so, takes the visitor's details and gets them to your team. It doesn't guess about prices, timelines or policies.

Do you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee results, so we don't. We measure your leads before and after, and you keep it only if the numbers are there.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

No. Our team does the full setup on every plan, and billing is month-to-month, so you're never locked in.

What happens after the trial?

You decide. Keep it month-to-month on the plan that fits, or we take it down.
