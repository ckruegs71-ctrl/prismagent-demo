Questions owners ask

What does PrismAgent Solutions do?

We build custom AI systems that take the hand-typed work out of the jobs that make a business money. That means reports and proposals that build themselves from your own data, dashboards pulled out of the software you already pay for, calls and leads answered day or night, and private AI for firms that can't send client data to the cloud. Every system is built inside your own tools and measured in dollars before and after.

Who do you work with?

Owner-led businesses across the United States, from one person to about 100, that run on spreadsheets, Word templates, a phone and an industry program. So far that includes home services and contractors, landscaping and lawn care, law firms, agriculture and commodity firms, recruiting and staffing, and real estate. If you're in another industry, Chad can tell you on a short call whether it's a fit.

Do you work with businesses outside Minnesota?

Yes. We're based in Lakeville, Minnesota, and we work with businesses anywhere in the United States.

Can AI build our reports, proposals or contracts?

Yes. We build systems that take your data and fill your own templates, word for word, so a report, proposal, contract or work order comes out ready for a person to check and send. The math runs in code, not in the AI, and someone on your team checks what matters before anything goes out.

Can you get the numbers out of the software we already use?

Often, yes. A lot of businesses pay for a program that holds their numbers but makes them hard to see. We pull that data into a dashboard or a weekly brief the owner actually reads. Whether it can be done depends on the software, so Chad checks yours on a short call.

Our client data can't go to the cloud. Can we still use AI?

Yes. For firms like law offices that can't send client data out, we build private AI that keeps the data in the building instead of sending it to an outside AI service. Chad scopes the setup with you on a call.

How does the free AI assessment work?

Start it from the assessment section of our website, then talk with Aria, our AI voice agent, whenever it suits you. She walks you through a short interview about how your business runs. She won't pitch you, and you get a written report within 2 business days.

Do you guarantee results?

No one can honestly guarantee results, so we don't. What we do is measure: we write down your numbers before we build and track them after, so you can see exactly what changed.
